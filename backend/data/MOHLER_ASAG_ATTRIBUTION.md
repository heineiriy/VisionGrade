# Mohler ASAG Dataset Attribution

- Dataset: Mohler ASAG, raw open-ended responses
- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Viswa45/ASAG
- License: CC BY 4.0
- Original authors: Michael Mohler, Razvan Bunescu, and Rada Mihalcea
- Paper: Mohler, Bunescu, and Mihalcea (2011), "Learning to Grade Short Answer Questions using Semantic Similarity Measures and Dependency Graph Alignments," ACL-HLT 2011, pp. 752-762. https://aclanthology.org/P11-1076/
- Accessed: 2026-10-02

The downloaded `mohler_asag_open.csv` contains open-ended answers with question IDs, student answers, instructor answers, and the mean of two human scores. Scores are rounded to the nearest integer during classifier training.
